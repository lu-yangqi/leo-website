# V8.1.3 --- Hero Identity Transition Refinement

## Task

Refine the V8.1 cinematic Hero transition after real browser testing.

The goal is to improve the relationship between portrait disappearance
and Leo signature appearance while preserving the existing Hero
architecture.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Main Goal

Maintain the identity sequence:

``` text
Portrait
    ↓
Center Focus
    ↓
Portrait Fade Out
    ↓
Leo Signature Reveal
    ↓
Homepage Content
```

The final experience should make the portrait feel like a meaningful
introduction, then allow the signature to become the final visual
identity.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Design Decision

The portrait should remain centered during the transition.

Do not move the portrait to another layout position.

The intended storytelling is:

``` text
A person appears

↓

The person becomes a visual memory

↓

The signature becomes the lasting identity
```

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Requirements

## Portrait Center Lock

Adjust the center-scale behavior.

Current issue:

-   The portrait becomes too small during the center phase.
-   The user loses visual connection with the person before the
    signature appears.

Target:

-   Keep the portrait recognizable during center lock.
-   Preserve the central composition.

Suggested adjustment:

``` text
centerScale:
0.34 → approximately 0.45–0.50
```

The final value should be tuned after visual review.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Portrait Fade Timing

Do not immediately fade the portrait after scaling.

New sequence:

``` text
01. Scale down

Portrait moves from large view to center focus.

↓

02. Hold

Portrait remains visible briefly.

↓

03. Fade

Portrait gradually disappears.

↓

04. Signature

Leo signature takes over.
```

The hold phase should create a clear transition moment.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Signature Transition

Preserve:

-   Existing Leo SVG asset
-   Existing signature reveal system
-   Existing motion controller

The signature should appear only after the portrait has mostly
disappeared.

Avoid:

-   Portrait and signature competing visually
-   Simultaneous full visibility

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Technical Constraints

Preserve:

-   Existing routes
